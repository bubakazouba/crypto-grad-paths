Crypto Grad Paths

PhD-first plan for theory crypto, Fall 2027. US PhD and master's routes, Summer 2027 internships, a dated apply-by timeline, and other routes. Data gathered 2026-11-06 from official pages and corrected after two independent reviews; every field links its source. Blank = not confirmed. Schools are sorted by fit to your topics (lower bounds, memory checking, ORAM, PIR, garbling, oblivious algorithms, lattices); the best-matched professors are listed under Other routes. Your shortlist, status and notes stay in this browser only.

Days left are counted from today's date on your device. "Priority" = early, round-1, scholarship or full-consideration date (usually the one to hit); "Final" = last date stated. "Ask for letters" rows are the deadline minus 4 weeks, a rule of thumb, not an official date. Dates whose year was not printed on the official page were assigned to the Fall 2027 cycle; each row's note says so.
